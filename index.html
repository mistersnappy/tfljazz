Every Train, Every Delay, Every Cancellation, a Note — TfL Jazz

Live London Underground arrivals from the TfL Unified API, sonified in real time. Each line has its own instrument and its own track below; a dot crosses the rail as a real train approaches its next station, and a note fires the moment it arrives.
